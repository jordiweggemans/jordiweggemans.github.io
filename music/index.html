About

I write and produce music, focusing on pop, neo-soul and cinematic music, with occasional genre experiments. I play guitar, bass and piano, and I care a lot about feel: I try to keep human performance as a cornerstone of every project.

I also release instrumental guitar music under the artist name Athelnee.
